\subsection{Preliminary benchmark results}

The archived 50-case sweep used 100 N linear static loading, a fixed 20-ply
3.50 mm laminate, and a 40 by 6 SHELL181 mesh. The surrogate split comprised
37 training and 13 held-out cases; five analytical reference observations were
selected from training data only. The quadratic baseline gave 0.977\% MAPE
against held-out FEA responses. The raw Gaussian process gave 0.139\% MAPE
against the analytical reference, and sparse discrepancy correction gave
0.150\%. Thus correction did not improve MAPE in this run.
