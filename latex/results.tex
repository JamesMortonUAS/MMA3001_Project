\section{Results from the direct-stiffness calibration}
One analytical observation (case 109) gave $\widehat{s}_E=0.97609$. On 24 held-out geometries, MAPE changed from 5.323\% at nominal stiffness to 5.128\% after this centred calibration; RMSE increased from $2.668\times10^{-4}$ to $3.040\times10^{-4}$ m/N.

A follow-up sensitivity analysis tested 30 training-only calibration subsets for each of 1, 2, 3, and 5 observations, with the same held-out geometry set. Mean MAPE worsened for every count (+1.521, +0.830, +0.895, and +0.535 percentage points, respectively), and only 30--36.7\% of subsets improved MAPE. The fitted stiffness scale became less variable with more observations, but prediction accuracy did not improve on average. See Table~\ref{tab:calibration-sensitivity} and Figure~\ref{fig:calibration-sensitivity} in the main report; raw outputs are in \texttt{results/calibration\_sensitivity\_2026-10-17/}.
