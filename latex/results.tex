\section{Results from the direct-stiffness calibration}
The fitted shared effective stiffness scale was $\widehat{s}_E=0.97609$, using one analytical calibration observation. On 24 held-out geometries, MAPE changed from 5.323\% at nominal stiffness to 5.128\% after calibration, while RMSE increased from $2.668\times10^{-4}$ to $3.040\times10^{-4}$ m/N. The results show a mixed effect across metrics; see Table~\ref{tab:current-results} in the main report.
